\section{Additional results: counterfactual test sets}\label{sec:s_results_a}

The tables in this section follow the order of Sections~\ref{subsec:res_main} to~\ref{subsec:res_external}.
Measures are those of Section~\ref{subsec:measures}: lesion images correct (share of original and control-edited images answered correctly), erased-lesion claims (share of erased images on which the finding was reported), lesion-free claims (share of unedited lesion-free images on which a finding was reported), the evidence sensitivity gap (ESG) and net utility.
Unless a caption says otherwise, brackets are 95\% bootstrap intervals over cases (2{,}000 resamples), differences are paired by case, and ``denied'' means answered ``absent'' or referred.
The three reference models are \gem, \opus{} and \gpt.
A first-stage model is a model after the first of the three training stages.
