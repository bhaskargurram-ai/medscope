Image reading study

Thank you for taking part. You will see 216 medical images (CT, MRI, X-ray, ultrasound and colonoscopy) from public research datasets. Some images are unmodified and some have been digitally edited. For each image you answer two short questions.

  • Time: about 2 hours in total. You do not need to finish in one sitting.
  • Saving: every answer is saved automatically as you go. You can close the page at any time and open your link again later, on this or another device, to continue where you stopped.
  • Privacy: the images contain no patient-identifying information. Your answers are stored under a reader code, not your name, and are used only to evaluate the study images.

About you

How to answer

For each image, two questions:

  1. Has this image been digitally edited? Rate from definitely real to definitely edited. An edited image may have had a region altered or filled in by software.
  2. The clinical question shown under the image (for example, "Is there a polyp?"). Answer Yes, No or Cannot tell from what you see in this image.
  • Answer from the image alone; there is no other clinical information.
  • Please answer the clinical question as you would in practice, even if you suspect the image was edited.
  • Tap or click the image to enlarge it; in the enlarged view, click again to zoom further.
  • An optional note box lets you say briefly what you see (for example, where a finding is). Notes are welcome but never required.
  • You can go back to change an answer; the latest answer counts.
